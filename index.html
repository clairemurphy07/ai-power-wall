⚠️ Economic Risks: The Valuation Re-Pricing Dilemma

When hundreds of billions in capital expenditures collide with multi-year grid backlogs, systemic financial vulnerabilities arise. A political slowdown risks triggering a severe market repricing across three key dimensions:

01 / Inventory Glut

Idle Silicon Backlog

Advanced GPUs sit in warehouses waiting for air permits and substations, creating sudden shipment air pockets for chipmakers.

02 / Margin Compression

Sunk Capex Costs

If data centers cannot come online to generate revenue, massive infrastructure outlays turn into heavy sunk costs, squeezing hyperscaler margins.

03 / Capital Flight

Rotation to Efficiency

Wall Street capital rotates rapidly away from brute-force hardware scaling toward software quantization, model compression, and nuclear PPAs.
