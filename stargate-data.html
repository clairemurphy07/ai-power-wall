The Hybrid ERCOT Strategy

Unlike heavily regulated regional monopolies, Texas's independent ERCOT market structure allows developers to move faster through commercial queue arrangements. However, to prevent grid instability from multi-hundred-megawatt spikes, Stargate campuses integrate on-site, behind-the-meter gas microgrids powered by advanced GE Vernova and Siemens turbines directly on the property—mirroring the core resilience philosophy pioneered in Memphis.
