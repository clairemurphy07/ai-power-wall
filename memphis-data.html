Regulatory & Legal Milestones

  • The "364-Day Non-Road" Exemption: Trailers mounted on wheels were classified as temporary mobile engines to avoid immediate Title V Clean Air Act pre-construction permitting.
  • Federal DOJ Intervention (June 2026): The U.S. Department of Justice intervened in NAACP v. xAI, arguing that restricting the turbines threatened national security and presidential Article II authority.
  • Court Rejection & Fines (October 2026): Federal courts denied the DOJ's dismissal motion, allowing citizen lawsuits to proceed, while Mississippi regulators moved forward with up to $6.1 million in state penalties.
